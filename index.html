Five Gold Rings

Each page here grew from one 64-character hash: its palette, type, layout and every random flake or star. Each gold ring carries its seed as 256 engraved ticks, one per bit. Choose a ring to open that page.

    On the fifth day of Christmas: five gold rings. Ring ticks read clockwise from twelve o'clock; a long tick is a 1 bit.
