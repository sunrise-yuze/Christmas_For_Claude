Astrolabeof Midwinter

A brass astrolabe set for Bethlehem, 31°42′ north, showing the real sky of Christmas Eve. Its pierced star map, the rete, turns from six in the evening to six in the morning. At midnight the Winter Hexagon crosses the meridian in the south.
