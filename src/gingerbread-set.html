GENERAL NOTES

  1. All dimensions in millimetres.
  2. Trim pieces to template while warm.
  3. Royal icing to stiff peak. Cure 2 h before roof.
  4. Do not scale drawing. Use figured dimensions.
  5. Roll dough to 5 mm ±0.5. Re-roll scraps once only.
  6. Side walls sit between gable walls. Check corners square.
  7. Keep icing covered with a damp cloth when not in use.
  8. Candy loads to be applied symmetrically about the ridge.
  9. Lighting: battery mini-lights only. No open flame inside the structure.
  10. Chimney clearance for Santa to be verified on site.
  11. Store away from steam. Humidity softens joints.
